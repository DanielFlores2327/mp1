About Prime Coaching

Prime Coaching has an incredibly unique, exploitative strategy toward poker.

GTO Doesn't Matter

We heavily lean into exploits and never have our students touch the solver until they are already crushers. Balance is the enemy of profit.

Spot Experts

We have coaches that each specialize in specific spots such as 4-bet pots, OOP in wide vs. wide config, and short-stack ICM. Specializing is way better than generalization for coaching and our coaches spend years and years specializing.

Exploits > Leaks

We train our students to focus on exploiting the public leaks rather than fixing their own leaks. Eventually, you do want to fix your own leaks but students will make so much more playing an exploitable style.
